How it works

Three steps, all before the first warm-up.

  1. 1

    Plan

    Pick from 81 workout types, then set the day, the start time and how long it lasts. Each session is saved as an event in your own Calendar.

  2. 2

    Prep

    Open the session's brief for hydration, stretching and nutrition, and pack from a checklist suggested for that kind of workout.

  3. 3

    Go

    Home counts down to your next session and shows what is still left to pack. Turn on a reminder if you like one, from 15 minutes to 2 hours before.

Your bag, sorted

Every session gets a packing list suggested for its workout type. Tick things off as they go in the bag, add your own, and see at a glance how much is left.

Everything before the workout, in one app

Workout Prep doesn't track your training. It gets you ready for it.

  • Plan the weeks ahead

    See the coming weeks at a glance and add a session in a few taps. Search 81 workout types, from running and yoga to climbing and tennis.

  • A prep brief for each session

    Short suggestions on hydration, stretching and nutrition, fitted to the workout, its length and its start time.

    General fitness suggestions, not medical advice.

  • A packing checklist

    Suggested for the type of workout, with your own items added. Your progress shows on Home and in a widget.

  • A Home that knows what's next

    Your next session with a live countdown, how ready you are, your planning streak, and the things not to forget.

  • Your Calendar, your reminders

    Your plan lives in your own Calendar, and Workout Prep only touches the events it created. Optional reminders arrive 15 minutes to 2 hours before.

  • Widgets and Siri

    Four widgets: Next Session, Prep Brief, Bag Progress and This Week. Or ask Siri, “What’s my next workout in Workout Prep?”

Questions

Do I need an account?

No. Workout Prep has no account and no sign-in, and no server to sign in to.

Do I need Apple Intelligence for the prep brief?

No. When Apple's on-device model is available, it writes your brief. When it isn't, Workout Prep builds the brief from its own built-in rules. Either way, the brief is made on your device.

Is there a subscription?

No. Workout Prep is a one-time purchase. There is no subscription, nothing to unlock inside the app, and no ads.

Where does my data live?

On your iPhone and in your own Calendar. Each session is an event in your Calendar, and your settings and packing lists stay on your device. The developer holds none of your data.
